Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 04, 2026

At Dhenge Forensic Consultancy, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we handle data and evidence.

Data Collection

We collect information provided via our contact form and during the course of forensic investigations. This may include personal identification and case-specific data.

Evidence Security

All evidence submitted for analysis is stored in a secure, climate-controlled facility with strict chain-of-custody protocols.

Information Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law or authorized by you for legal proceedings.
