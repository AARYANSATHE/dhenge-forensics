Our Story

Integrity, Precision, and Scientific Excellence since 2010.

Dedicated to the Truth

Dhenge Forensic Consultancy was founded with a single mission: to provide the legal system and corporate world with unbiased, scientifically rigorous forensic analysis. Over the past 15 years, we have grown from a small laboratory into a multi-disciplinary consultancy recognized for our expertise and integrity.

Our team consists of seasoned experts from law enforcement, academia, and private research, ensuring a holistic approach to every case we handle. We believe that every detail matters and that science is the most powerful tool in the pursuit of justice.

Our Mission

To deliver precise forensic analysis that stands up to the highest levels of scrutiny, helping our clients navigate complex legal and technical challenges with confidence.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark for forensic excellence, continuously innovating and setting new standards for scientific investigation and expert testimony.
