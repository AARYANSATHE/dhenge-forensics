Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit evidence for analysis?
Evidence can be submitted in person at our secure facility or via registered courier. Please contact us first to receive a submission form and chain-of-custody instructions.
Are your experts available for court testimony?
Yes, all our lead experts are experienced in providing scientific testimony in both civil and criminal courts.
What is your typical turnaround time?
Turnaround times vary by discipline. Document examination typically takes 5-7 days, while complex digital forensics or toxicology may take up to 21 days.
